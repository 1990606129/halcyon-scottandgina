01 — Overview
Denver's best-kept
secret.

Scott and Gina stayed at Halcyon, a Cherry Creek hotel in Denver, to celebrate Gina's birthday. The collaboration showcased the property's rooftop pool and bar overlooking the Rocky Mountains, on-site dining at Local Jones, and the personalized service that made the birthday celebration feel truly special.

The content positioned Cherry Creek as the smart alternative to downtown Denver — walkable, full of great restaurants, and way less hectic. This "hidden gem" angle resonated strongly with an audience actively looking for authentic travel recommendations beyond the obvious picks.

“Honestly wasn't expecting much because I'd never heard of the neighborhood, but Cherry Creek might be one of the best kept secrets in Denver.”

Accommodation
Boutique Room — Cherry Creek
Hosted birthday stay at Halcyon, a Cherry Creek hotel. Modern design, comfortable rooms, and the kind of personalized service that made Gina's birthday celebration feel genuinely special. The staff went out of their way to create a memorable experience.
Dining
Local Jones
On-site dining at Local Jones — Halcyon's in-house restaurant. Solid food in a casual, inviting setting. A key content touchpoint that showcased the convenience and quality of the hotel's culinary offering without needing to leave the property.
Amenities
Rooftop Pool & Bar
The standout amenity — a rooftop pool and bar with panoramic views of the Rocky Mountains. This was the visual centerpiece of the content and the single biggest driver of engagement, saves, and shares. The mountain backdrop gave the property a unique selling point no downtown Denver hotel can match.

Cherry Creek, Denver
Situated in Cherry Creek — a walkable, upscale neighborhood with great restaurants that most visitors overlook in favor of downtown. The content's "skip downtown, stay here" positioning turned the neighborhood itself into a discovery, driving saves from an audience planning future Denver trips.
