01 — Overview
A hidden gem,
revealed.

Scott and Gina stayed at the Halcyon Hotel in Cherry Creek North, Denver — a luxury boutique hotel known for its vibrant rooftop scene, curated Gear Garage, and walkable neighborhood. The collaboration showcased the property's stylish rooms, rooftop dining at Rare Bird, and the unique amenities that set Halcyon apart from a typical hotel stay.

The content positioned Cherry Creek as an unexpected Denver highlight — a neighborhood most travelers overlook in favor of downtown. This "hidden gem" angle drove massive engagement, with nearly all interactions coming from non-followers discovering the property for the first time.

"Honestly wasn't expecting much because I'd never heard of the neighborhood, but Cherry Creek might be one of the best kept secrets in Denver."

04 — The Experience
Everything
provided.

A breakdown of the full hosted experience — from room to rooftop to neighborhood — that made this collaboration possible.

Accommodation
The Suite — Modern Luxury
Multi-night stay in one of the Halcyon's signature suites, featuring contemporary design, premium amenities, and floor-to-ceiling windows with Cherry Creek views. Full access to the hotel's curated amenities including the Gear Garage with complimentary bikes, skateboards, and adventure equipment.
Dining
Rare Bird & Quality Italian
Dining at the Halcyon's rooftop Rare Bird bar — one of Denver's most sought-after rooftop experiences — plus Quality Italian, the on-site restaurant. A cornerstone of the content, showcasing the caliber of the culinary and cocktail program.
Amenities
Gear Garage & Rooftop Pool
Complete access to the Halcyon's signature amenities — the Gear Garage stocked with bikes, skateboards, and outdoor gear, plus the rooftop pool and sundeck. This "adventure-meets-luxury" angle was key to the content's appeal and differentiated the Halcyon from typical hotel stays.
Location
Cherry Creek North, Denver
Situated in Denver's premier shopping and dining district — a walkable, upscale neighborhood that most visitors overlook. The "hidden gem" positioning of Cherry Creek drove strong saves and shares from an audience actively planning Denver trips.
